Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What We Collect

SpendSmart collects the following data to provide expense tracking functionality:

  • Financial Information: Expenses, income, budgets, and categories you enter
  • Account Data: Your unique sync key (generated locally on your device)
  • App Preferences: Theme, currency, language, and notification settings
  • Technical Data: Crash reports and performance metrics (via Firebase Crashlytics)

We do not collect: personal identifiers (name, email, phone), location data, contacts, or browsing history.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Store and sync your expense data across your devices
  • Generate spending insights and reports for you
  • Provide budget alerts and notifications
  • Improve app stability and performance

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
  • Sync Key: Your data is protected by a unique 100-bit sync key (ss-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) generated on your device using cryptographically secure random values
  • Access Control: Firestore security rules enforce that only your sync key can access your data. No listing or enumeration is possible.
  • Infrastructure: Hosted on Google Firebase with SOC 2, ISO 27001 compliance

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties.

Data may be processed by our infrastructure providers (Google Firebase) solely to provide the service. Google's processing of IP addresses is governed by their privacy policy.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Data is retained while your account is active
  • You can delete all your data from within the app at any time
  • You can request deletion by emailing support@spendsmart.app
  • After deletion, data is removed from active storage within 30 days
  • If you lose your sync key, data cannot be recovered (by design)

6. Local Data & PDFs

Expense reports and PDFs are generated locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly choose to share them.

7. Children's Privacy

SpendSmart is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@spendsmart.app
