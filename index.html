How your data stays yours

No name, email or phone number needed. One key that only you hold.

  1. Your device makes a key

    SpendSmart generates a unique 100-bit sync key on your device, using cryptographically secure random values.

  2. Your data is encrypted

    Everything is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest, on Google Firebase infrastructure.

  3. Only your key opens it

    Security rules let only your sync key reach your data. Nobody can list or browse other people's entries.

  4. Keep the key safe

    Your key is how your data follows you across devices. If it's lost, the data can't be recovered, by design.

Built so only your key opens your data.

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Email spendsmartapp.help@gmail.com.

Do I need to create an account?

No name, email or phone number is needed. SpendSmart generates a sync key on your device, and that key is how your data is found and protected.

What happens if I lose my sync key?

Your data can't be recovered. That's by design: we can't reset the key for you, so keep it somewhere safe.

Can SpendSmart read my finances?

Your data isn't end-to-end encrypted. It's encrypted in transit and at rest by Google, and database rules let only your sync key reach it. It isn't encrypted on your device before upload, so someone with administrative access to the project's Firebase console could technically view stored data. We don't sell, rent or share your data, and Google Firebase processes it only to run the service.

Which devices does it run on?

SpendSmart is currently available for Android 7.0 and newer, from Google Play or as a direct APK.

Google Play or the APK?

Google Play is the easiest option and keeps the app updated for you. The APK is for people who prefer to install directly. Compare its SHA-256 checksum with the one on this page before you install.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete all of it from inside the app at any time, or email spendsmartapp.help@gmail.com. After deletion, data is removed from active storage within 30 days.

What do crash reports include?

Crash reports and performance metrics are collected through Firebase Crashlytics to help fix bugs. SpendSmart doesn't collect location data, contacts or browsing history.

Is SpendSmart free?

Yes. The app is free to use and has no ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can add and view entries without a connection, and they sync when you're back online.

Can I export my data?

Yes. In Profile, open Your data and choose Export report to create a monthly PDF summary you can save or share.

How do I report a bug or get help?

Email spendsmartapp.help@gmail.com, or open an issue on GitHub.
